About the System

About Attendance System

Classroom presence, without the paperwork

This system helps teaching staff record who is in class each day, spot patterns early, and export clean records. Add students once, mark Present / Absent / Late per day, and review per-student rates under Reports.

All data is stored locally in this browser. No account, no server, nothing leaves the device.

  • Dashboard - today's headline numbers at a glance.
  • Students - the roster: add, search and remove.
  • Attendance - the daily register with per-date history.
  • Reports - totals and rates computed from saved days.
